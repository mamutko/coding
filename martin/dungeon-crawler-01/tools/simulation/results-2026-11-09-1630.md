- **No life regeneration** (`REGEN = 0`, no regen affix, the Bone Talisman gives crit instead).
- **Fewer monsters per room:** an even draw from 1 to `min(7, 3 + ⌊(d − 1)/2⌋)` (+1 half the time in rooms of

  new type per room on its first depth (skeletons and spiders exempt).

- **Big rooms** at depths 3, 5, 7, …: boss-room sized, 2 grey banners, 5–7 (+1 per 2 depths after 3) easier

## Conclusions

1. **Damage per level roughly halved at depths 1–4**, as intended. Depth 1 is now gentle (rooms of 1–3, nobody
   dies), and depth 3–4 take less than half the damage they did.
2. **But deaths barely fell, because healing fell too.** Without regeneration, life comes back only from potions
   (3–5 found per level, 35 % each) and level-ups (20 %). Fewer monsters also means less XP, so the hero is one
   level lower at every depth from 3 on, with less life and fewer level-up heals. At depth 3 the hero takes 3.1×
   its max life and can heal back only about 1 + 3.2 × 0.35 + 0.2 ≈ 2.3× (full life on arrival, all potions,
   one level-up). Potions drunk now equal or exceed potions found from depth 2 on, so the stock runs dry.
3. **Rooms with a champion are now the main killer** (about half of all deaths at every depth from 2 on). A
   champion has 2.5× life and 1.5× damage and brings 2–3 escorts, and with smaller normal rooms it stands out
   even more.
4. **The big room is a real fight but fair:** about 0.5 deaths per run at depths 3 and 5, close to a champion
   room. In play, fighting it from a doorway makes it much easier than this model, where the whole pack attacks
   at once.
5. **Depths 5–6 jump** (5.1× and 5.9× max life) because Pit Demons arrive (about 25 % of the damage) and, at depth 6,
   the boss (12 %).
6. The **lone demon at depth 4** costs little (9 % of that depth's damage) and works as a warning.

## Recommendations

Healing has to replace regeneration, and the champion packs are the next lever:

1. **More healing:** raise potion drops (monsters 6.75 % → about 12 %, urns 9 % → 15 %, chests 22.5 % → 40 %), so
   potions found clearly exceed potions drunk. Alternatives: a stronger health potion (35 % → 50 %), a full heal
   on taking the stairs, or rare healing shrines / fountains on a level (healing in exchange for exploring, not
   for waiting).
2. **Gentler champions:** 1–2 escorts instead of 2–3, or champions from depth 3 instead of 2.
3. **Re-check XP:** with fewer monsters the hero levels more slowly (6 on arrival at depth 6, was 7). Monster XP or
   the level curve (`120 · L^1.7`) could be eased to keep the old pace.

Goal unchanged: damage per level at about 3–4× max life through depth 6 (met through depth 4), with potions
found ≥ potions drunk (not met).
