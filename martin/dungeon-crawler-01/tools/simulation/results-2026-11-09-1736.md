## Conclusions

1. **The game is much harder at every simulated depth.** The flatter curve only catches up with the old one at depth
   8, so through depth 7 monsters are stronger than before: 50 % more life and damage at depth 1, 18 % at depth 5.
2. **Depth 1 changed the most.** A skeleton now has 39 life and hits for 6.8 on average; the starting hero (86 life,
   no armor, 12.5 DPS) needs 3.1 s to kill one and dies to one in 15 s (was 2.1 s and 23 s). The hero drinks 3.9
   potions at depth 1 while finding 2.5, so it uses up its 3 starting potions on the first level.
3. **Depth 3** (the skeleton big room) is again the hardest of the early depths, and depths 5–6 are back at 5.5× max
   life.
4. In the long run the change does what it should: from depth 9 on, monsters grow more slowly than before.

## Recommendations

If the flatter curve is the goal but the early game should stay as it was in the third pass:

1. **A smaller depth-1 bump:** `MON_BASE = 1.2` with `MON_SCALE = 1.15` crosses the old curve at depth 4 and stays
   within ±20 % of it through depth 7.
2. **Or a stronger start for the hero** (e.g. a better starting weapon or some starting armor), keeping `MON_BASE = 1.5`.
3. **Or more health potions at the start** (e.g. 5 instead of 3).
