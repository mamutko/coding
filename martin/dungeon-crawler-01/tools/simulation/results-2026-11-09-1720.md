## Conclusions

1. **Depths 1–4 are now easy to moderate:** 1.1–2.1× max life of damage per level and 0–23 % of runs with a death
   in this pessimistic model. Depth 3 is the hardest of them, because of its skeleton big room (79 % of that depth's
   damage comes from skeletons).
2. **But the hero is weaker on arrival:** champions were a large source of XP (×3) and of good loot (item level
   d + 1, rarity boost). Without them at depths 2–4, the hero arrives a level lower at depths 2–5, and at depth 5
   it has 34 armor instead of 57 and about 10 % less DPS.
3. **Depth 5 is now a cliff.** Everything arrives at once: the first champions (with their escorts, for all three
   depth-1 types), the first Pit Demons, and the second big room. Damage jumps from 1.7× to 3.9× max life, and
   champion rooms cause half of the depth's deaths. Depth 6 adds the boss.

## Recommendations

1. **Spread out depth 5:** let champions start earlier for the depth-1 types, e.g. `CHAMP_AFTER = 2` (champions from
   depth 3 for skeletons, ghouls and spiders; wraiths from 5, Pit Demons from 7), or give the depth-1 types a
   special start at depth 3 or 4.
2. **Or give champions fewer escorts** (1–2) when they first appear, so the first champion rooms are a step up
   rather than a wall.
3. If the hero should keep its old pace at depths 2–4, raise monster XP a little (e.g. +20 %), since champions no
   longer supply it.

Goal: damage per level at about 3–4× max life through depth 6 (now met at every depth, with depth 6 at 4.1×), and
potions found ≥ potions drunk (met except at depth 3).
