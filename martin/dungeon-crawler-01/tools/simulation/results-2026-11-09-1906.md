## Conclusions

1. **The potion supply now clearly exceeds use at every depth**, about 1–2 potions to spare per level, so a careful
   player builds a reserve for the hard rooms.
2. **Deaths fell by a third to a half** at depths 3–10 in the model; no depth is above 36 % of runs with a death.
3. In real play, slower monsters also let the hero retreat and pick fights, which the model can't show; the real game
   should feel easier than these numbers.

## Recommendations

Play-test again. If it now feels too easy, take back part of the potion bump first (e.g. `POTION_ODDS = 1.1`).
