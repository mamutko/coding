## Conclusions

1. **Every depth is now at or below the 3–4× target**, from 1.3× (depths 2 and 4) to 3.6× (depth 9), and deaths fell
   by about half from depth 4 on.
2. **The depth-9 cliff is now a step:** 2.5× → 3.6× (it was 3.5× → 5.3×). It still comes from the same causes
   (Pit Demons lose their room limit and become champions, the room cap reaches 7), but it is no longer a wall.
3. **The curve has a clear rhythm:** easy depths 1–2, a bump at 3 (skeleton big room), easy 4, a step at 5 (champions,
   Pit Demons), steady 5–8, a step at 9.
4. **Watch for the late game getting too easy.** One on one the hero now outgrows the monsters quickly: at depth 10 it
   kills a skeleton in 0.8 s, and a lone skeleton needs 60 s to kill it. What keeps late depths dangerous is
   numbers (champion packs cause most deaths from depth 5), not individual monsters.
5. Potions found exceed potions drunk at every depth.

## Recommendations

1. **Play-test depths 1–6 now**; the simulation says they are in a good place, and the model can't judge how fights
   feel.
2. If later depths feel too easy in play, **raise the number or size of packs** (e.g. room cap, champion escorts)
   rather than individual monster strength, since that's where the late-game challenge already comes from.
