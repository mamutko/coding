## Conclusions

- **Mild alone makes depths 3–13 about three times as deadly** (33 deaths per 15-depth run instead of 12). The hero's
  armor and damage lag behind: at depth 7 the median hero has armor 41 instead of 61 and 38 DPS instead of 52.
- **Slower monster growth per depth (11 % or 10 %) mainly helps the deep levels**: it compounds, so it changes little
  at depths 4–9, where the gap opens.
- **Bringing the early armor and magic items back (c)** isn't enough either: the later tiers still lag.
- **(b) weaker monsters from the start** (depth-1 multiplier 1.05 instead of 1.2) **plus 11 % per depth** brings the
  total back to the v51 level (11.8 against 11.7), a little easier at depths 2–4 and a little harder at 6–9.
