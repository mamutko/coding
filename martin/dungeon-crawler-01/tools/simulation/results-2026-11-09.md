200 runs, depths 1–6, `--travel 40`, `--pot 0.35`, `--melee 5`. The numbers come from two 200-run passes with
the same settings: the first two tables are from the first pass, and pack sizes and damage sources are from the
second (it re-ran the model with damage tracking added; the shared numbers agreed within a few percent).

## Conclusions

**One on one, the hero gets stronger than the monsters at every depth.** The time to kill a skeleton falls from
2.1 s to 1.35 s, and a lone skeleton needs longer and longer to kill the hero (23 s → 36 s). Gear, about one level
per depth and armor (0 → 39 % damage cut) more than make up for the monsters' +22 % per depth.

**The game gets hard because of how many monsters there are and how little healing there is.** The causes, in order:

1. **The Pit Demon at depth 3.** It has about 3× a skeleton's life and 2.3× its hit, and arrives when the hero is
   only level 4. From depth 3 on, demons cause about 30 % of all damage taken, though they are the rarest type
   (weight 1 of 11).
2. **Champion packs from depth 2.** A champion has 2.5× life and 1.5× damage, plus 2–3 escorts. This makes rooms
   of up to 13–16 monsters that all fight at once. Champions cause about 30 % of the damage from depth 2 on.
3. **Healing doesn't keep up.** Damage taken per level grows from 2.4× the hero's max life at depth 1 to 6.6× at
   depth 3 and 8.6× at depth 6. Healing barely grows:
   - 3–6 potions found per level, each healing 35 % (about 1–2 bars);
   - regen of 0.2 % of max life per second, so about 8 minutes for a full bar;
   - one level-up per depth, which heals 20 %.

   The hero has potions left over at depth 1 but runs dry from depth 3 on.
4. **More monsters per level.** The count goes from 26 to 68 by depth 6: levels get more rooms, and each room gets
   `floor(depth / 3)` more monsters.

## Recommendations

The most targeted first; try one or two at a time and re-run the simulation:

1. **Delay or soften the Pit Demon:** `minD: 4` instead of 3, or a weaker base (e.g. 55 life, 6–10 hit). This
   removes the depth-3 spike directly.
2. **More healing:** raise potion drop chances (monsters 6.75 %, chests 22.5 %, urns 9 %), or let health potions
   heal more deeper down. Or add faster regen out of combat (e.g. 2 % of max life per second after 5 s without
   taking damage), which helps between rooms without making fights easier.
3. **Smaller champion packs:** 1–2 escorts instead of 2–3, or a cap on monsters per room.
4. **Slower growth in monster count:** e.g. `floor(depth / 4)` extra per room instead of `floor(depth / 3)`.

Goal to aim for: damage taken per level at about 3–4× max life through depth 6, with potions found ≥ potions drunk.
