- **No item levels:** each type has fixed stats; progress comes from type ladders (new types unlocking down to depth

## Conclusions

1. **Depths 3–8 got harder** (deaths about doubled in the model); depth 10 is about the same, and deeper the hero
   now outgrows the old one (118.7 DPS at depth 10, was 105.9).
2. **The main cause is the later rarity gates.** Magic items now start at depth 3 and rares at 6, so the hero has
   fewer affixes (less life, armor and damage) through the middle depths: at depth 6, 28 less life and 19 % less DPS.
   The type ladders themselves roughly match the old item-level curve at each type's unlock depth.
3. **The hero's power now comes in steps** (when a new type or affix tier unlocks) instead of a smooth curve, which
   shows as uneven death rates (depth 8 at 38 % between 58 % and 44 %).

## Recommendations

If the middle depths feel too hard in play (the model is pessimistic):

1. **More magic items:** raise `MAGIC_ODDS` from 0.25 to about 0.35 (10.5 % magic per plain drop), to make up for the
   later start.
2. **Or stronger middle tiers:** raise the ladder types unlocking at depths 3–7 by about 10 %.
3. Re-run the simulation after either change.
