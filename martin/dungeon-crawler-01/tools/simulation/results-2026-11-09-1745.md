## Conclusions

1. **Depths 7–8 hold steady** at about 3.5–3.8× max life of damage per level, the same as depths 5–6. The flatter
   monster curve (+15 % per depth) and the hero's growth keep pace.
2. **One on one, the hero pulls ahead from depth 6.** The time to kill a skeleton keeps falling (1.5 s → 1.06 s),
   and a lone skeleton needs 40+ s to kill the hero (19 s at depth 1). The hero's DPS grows about 20–35 % per depth,
   more than the monsters' +15 %. The hero's armor stops gaining ground at about 55 % damage cut from depth 7: it
   keeps rising (112 → 150), but the formula's denominator grows with monster level.
3. **Depth 9 is the next cliff: 5.3× max life (from 3.5×), 83 % of runs with a death.** Three things meet there:
   - **Pit Demons lose their room limit** (L + 4 = 9) and **can be champions** from 9, bringing 2–3 demon escorts.
     Demons and champions together cause about half the damage from depth 9.
   - **The room cap rises to 7** monsters (cap `min(7, 3 + ⌊(d − 1)/2⌋)` reaches 7 at depth 9), and levels get more
     rooms (62 monsters per level, up from 52).
   - **Champion rooms cause most deaths:** 1.6–1.9 per run at depths 9–10, double depth 8.
4. **Healing keeps up at depths 5–10:** potions found (4.6–6.7) exceed potions drunk (3.6–5.2) at every depth.
5. **Boss rooms are fair** (0.2–0.3 deaths per run), and **the big rooms are a moderate fight** at the depths that
   have one (odd depths: 0.2–0.3 deaths per run).

## Recommendations

1. **Soften depth 9:** demon champions later (e.g. no Pit Demon champions until depth 11), or fewer escorts for a
   demon champion (1–2), or at most 1 escort when a type first becomes a champion.
2. **Slow down the room cap:** e.g. `min(6, 3 + ⌊(d − 1)/3⌋)` reaches 6 at depth 10 instead of 7 at depth 9.
3. Otherwise **the long-run curve looks healthy:** damage per level is flat at 3.3–3.8× from depth 5 to 8, and the
   hero's power outgrows the monsters' one on one.
