## Conclusions

1. **The flatter curve works.** Depths 1–2 are a little harder than with the original curve (a real but survivable
   start: 2 % and 11 % of runs with a death), depth 4 is the same, and depths 5–6 are easier (3.4× and 3.2× max
   life instead of 3.9× and 4.1×). Every depth is now within the 3–4× target or below it.
2. **Potions found ≥ potions drunk at every depth except 3** (2.9 found, 3.0 drunk).
3. **Depth 3 is the bump in the early game** (45 % of runs with a death, 78 % of the damage from skeletons): its big
   room of 5–7 skeletons hits a level-3 hero. In play, fighting it from a doorway helps a lot.
4. **Depths 5–6 are still the hardest** (61–66 % of runs with a death): the first champions and Pit Demons at 5, the
   boss at 6. But the gap to depth 4 is now about 2× the damage, down from 2.3×.
5. From depth 7 on the new curve grows more slowly than the old one (0.84× at depth 7, 0.71× at 10). Depths beyond 6
   haven't been simulated yet (`--depths 10`).

## Recommendations

1. **Leave depths 1–4 as they are.**
2. **If depth 3 feels too hard in play:** 4–6 monsters in its big room instead of 5–7, or a doorway-friendly layout.
3. **To smooth depth 5:** champions 2 depths after a type appears (`CHAMP_AFTER = 2`) instead of 4, so they start at
   depth 3 and don't all arrive together with the Pit Demons.
4. **Simulate deeper** (`--depths 10`) to check the long-run balance of the flatter curve.
