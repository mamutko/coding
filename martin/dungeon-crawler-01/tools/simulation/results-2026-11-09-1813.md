## Conclusions

1. **Bosses got tougher, but only moderately:** they cause a third more of the damage, and boss-room deaths rose from
   about 0.04–0.13 to 0.06–0.13 per run. The fight lasts longer, so the boss gets more swings in.
2. **A boss room is now about as deadly as one champion room** (a champion room costs 0.07–0.14 deaths, the boss room
   0.06–0.13). Champion packs still cause more deaths per level, because there are 3–5 of them.
3. **The rest is unchanged** within normal random variation: every depth stays at or below 3.6× max life of damage
   per level.
4. The simulated hero always drinks iron skin for the boss; a player who doesn't will find the boss noticeably harder.

## Recommendations

1. To make the boss the clear highlight of a level, raise its **damage** rather than its life (e.g. ×2.2 instead of
   ×1.8), or give it **more guards** (2–4): more life mostly makes the fight longer.
2. Otherwise leave it and play-test.
