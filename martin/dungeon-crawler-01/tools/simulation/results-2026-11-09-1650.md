## Conclusions

1. **Depths 1–4 are now in a good place.** The hero arrives one level higher everywhere (back to the baseline's
   pace, and a level ahead at depth 2), takes 2.0–2.5× its max life per level, and deaths roughly halved. The model
   is pessimistic (no doorway fighting, retreating or kiting), so a 40 % chance of one death per level in the model
   means a careful player will usually make it.
2. **The healing gap is closing.** A health potion now heals 44 %, and potions found (2.6–5.1 per level) about equal
   potions drunk. Supply is still tight from depth 3 on.
3. **Iron skin helps, but supply limits it.** Only 25 % of potion drops are iron skin, so the hero has about one per
   level for the hard rooms. Champion rooms remain the main killer (about 60 % of deaths).
4. **Depths 5–6 are the next wall** (4.0× and 4.8× max life, 77–80 % of runs with a death): Pit Demons arrive at 5
   (23–28 % of the damage), and the boss at 6 (11 %).

## Recommendations

1. **Gentler champions** (1–2 escorts instead of 2–3, or from depth 3): the biggest single source of deaths.
2. **Soften depths 5–6:** a weaker Pit Demon (e.g. 55 life, 6–10 hit) or at most 1–2 per room, or more potion drops
   from depth 5.
3. Optionally, **more iron skin potions** (e.g. 35 % of potion drops instead of 25 %), so each hard room can have one.

Goal: damage per level at about 3–4× max life through depth 6 (met through depth 4; depths 5–6 at 4.0–4.8×), and
potions found ≥ potions drunk (about even).
