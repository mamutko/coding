## Planned features (TODO)

- Dungeon crawler: a single hero against monsters.
- Isometric view, camera centered on the player.
- Mouse controls: left click walks to the clicked spot or actions an item (open chest/door, pick up item); right click attacks (melee or ranged depending on the equipped weapon).
- Items to find and equip: armor pieces, weapons (swords and axes for melee, bows for ranged), rings and amulets. Only one weapon equipped at a time. Every item has stats or boosts the hero's stats.
- Equip overlay (partly transparent) where items are equipped from the inventory; the game keeps running while it is open.
- Hotkeys 1-9 and 0 can be assigned in the equip screen to quickly switch weapons.
- Damage per blow depends on hero/monster stats and their weapons. Everyone has health points that slowly regenerate; the hero's health is always visible.
- The dungeon continues indefinitely and monsters get progressively stronger.
- The hero earns XP for killing monsters and actioning items and levels up at XP checkpoints.
- Gothic-style art generated with AI, including 8-direction walking sprite sequences for the hero and monsters.
