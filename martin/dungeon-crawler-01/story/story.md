# Crypt of Endless Night — the story

> The game's story, in the dialogue format described in README.md, Dialogue. Compile it into story.js with
> `node tools/story/build.js` (from the game folder). Nothing is written yet: until a character here has a
> conversation, the game behaves as before (a click on a Keeper of the Crypt opens the market).
>
> Characters the game already places, and their ids:
> - the Keepers of the Crypt: aldric, borin, mirela, tobias (one per stall, every third depth)
> - monsters: boss (any boss), champion (any champion), or the type: skeleton, ghoul, spider, wraith, demon
>
> A character needs a `## Character:` section only for settings (name, starts, talk, leave) or @leave / @back lines.
> See demo.md for a worked example of every feature.
